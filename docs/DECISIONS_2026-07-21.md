# Human product decisions — 2026-07-21

This file records product boundaries selected for the WhyRight entry. It is not
a claim that implementation is already complete.

1. Submit a completely separate Education project instead of modifying or
   rebranding the previously submitted CrossReady project.
2. Focus on teachers and tutors practicing diagnostic questions, not on a
   general-purpose student tutor.
3. Train the specific "correct answer, wrong reason" moment.
4. Limit a round to three questions so the demo has a measurable constraint.
5. Pre-author the misconception, answer key, candidate diagnoses, and evidence
   rules; GPT-5.6 may not invent the grading target.
6. Use GPT-5.6 for the simulated student's counterfactual belief behavior and
   Structured Outputs, while keeping final scoring deterministic.
7. Use fictional students and synthetic scenarios only. Store no real student
   data.
8. Build only two scenarios before the deadline. Cut breadth before weakening
   the scoring boundary or replacing the live GPT-5.6 path.
9. Keep the interface English-first for judges and complete a full round in
   under 90 seconds without login.
10. Keep entrant identity data out of Git and the public repository.
